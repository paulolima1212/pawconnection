# Paw Connection — Play Store full description (en-US)

Paw Connection is a social app for dog owners. Create a profile for you and your dog, find people nearby, share moments on the feed, and chat with new friends.

**Meet nearby owners**
Use the map and discovery feed to find dog owners around you. Filter by distance and interests such as walks, parks, and playdates.

**Share your pack**
Post photos and updates, comment, and keep up with people you connect with.

**Chat with matches and friends**
Start conversations after a connection, react to messages, and keep your pup’s social life in one place.

**Your data, your choice**
Location is used only for discovery and distance. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account.
