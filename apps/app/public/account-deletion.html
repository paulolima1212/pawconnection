How to delete your Paw Connection account

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Google Play requires apps that create accounts to let you delete that account and associated data. Paw Connection does this in the app. No support ticket is required.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Paw Connection and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Deletion is permanent.

This removes your profile, pet details, photos we stored for you, posts, comments, chats, connection requests, and location data.

If you cannot open the app

Email plima12121984@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will complete the request as soon as reasonably possible.
