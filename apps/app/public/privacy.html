Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Paw Connection (“we”, “us”) is a social app for dog owners. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. Contact: plima12121984@gmail.com.

Who this app is for

Paw Connection helps people meet nearby dog owners, share posts, and chat. It is intended for adults. Do not create an account for a child.

Data we collect

  • Account: email, password (stored as a hash), display name, handle.
  • Profile: bio, age, gender, phone (optional), pet details and photos.
  • Location: approximate and precise location when you allow it, used for the discovery map, nearby suburbs, and distance filters.
  • User content: posts, comments, photos, and chat messages.
  • Device: basic app diagnostics needed to operate the service.

How we use data

We use this information to create your account, show profiles and posts, enable chat and connection requests, personalize nearby discovery, and keep the community safe. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Profile information you publish is visible to other users of Paw Connection. We use hosting, database, and file-storage providers to run the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos — profile pictures and post images, only when you choose to upload.
  • Location — map, nearby discovery, and distance filters, only while you use those features.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). That removes your profile, pet details, photos we store for you, posts, comments, chats, and location. You can also email us at plima12121984@gmail.com. See How to delete your account.

Security

Production traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. No method of transmission is 100% secure; please use a unique password.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
